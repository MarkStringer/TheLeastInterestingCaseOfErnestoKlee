# Concordance of Characters

## Principal Characters

**Ernesto Klee**
Former detective, protagonist. Pronounces his name "Clay" (everyone else says it to rhyme with "free"). Wardrobe full of thermals. Addicted to buses, burgers (Billy's Burgers, always the same order), and four-shot coffees. Ex-wife has left but is unaccounted for. Suffers from 4:44am dread. Now working as a live-in security guard in a deconsecrated Unitarian chapel in Kings Norton, Birmingham.
_Chapters: 1–14_

**Jackie Spanner**
Klee's former stakeout partner and closest friend. Tall, giant size-twelve feet, chronically untidy, always eating prawn cocktail crisps. Obsessed with catching corrupt police. Loved horror movies and prawns. Found dead on his icy patio – barefoot, in his wife's dressing gown, with a shotgun. Klee is certain he was murdered. Had texted Klee hours before his death: "The call's coming from inside the house."
_Chapters: 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 14_

**Yannick Pausing**
Klee's former sergeant (corrects Klee at the end: he was actually promoted to inspector just before he resigned). Still in contact with Klee via covert YouTube cypher — Klee posts an encoded message on an agreed video and Pausing calls him back. Arranged the Birmingham chapel job as cover for Klee. Owns a corgi called Maggie, left behind by a former partner.
_Chapters: 2, 4, 6, 13, 14_

## The Chapel Household

**Ulysses Boht**
The old man who owned and lived in the chapel at 47 Pachelbel Road. Died approximately two weeks before Klee's arrival. Filled the chapel with antiques, cigarette cards, paintings, and a Victorian Berber tent. Behind the chapel: a Sunday school room turned labyrinthine library. The freezer in the cellar contains a frozen body. By Chapter 14, it is established that "Boht doesn't exist" – the name is a false identity.
_Chapters: 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 12, 13, 14_

**Mrs Ting**
Neighbour at 45a Pachelbel Road. Elderly, fierce, convinced the people next door are Germans who are always cooking German curries. Widowed (husband Jimmy died 1995 – "an awful man and I'm glad he's dead"). Was Boht's cleaner; cleans and does laundry at the chapel. Younger-looking than Klee expects when he finally sees her properly.
_Chapters: 3, 7, 8, 9, 11_

**Lillian Jones (née Ting) / Lilly**
Mrs Ting's daughter, lives with her at 45a. A lot of red hair, pale skin, freckles. Works shifts (nurse? Not specified). Married name Jones – says the name is "about the only good thing I got from my husband." Meets Klee in a coffee shop in town.
_Chapters: 3, 5, 7_

**Jimmy Ting**
Mrs Ting's late husband. Died 1995. Mrs Ting is not grief-stricken.
_Chapter: 9_

## The Criminal Case

**Darren Oliver Brand ("Dobby" Brand)**
Master criminal. Mastermind of the Cadfield Farm heist (early 1990s) and the Collaigne-Schotts bullion robbery (October 1994 – 247 kilos of gold, current value ~£25 million). Nicknamed "the General" for military-style planning. Signature: no violence, hospital anaesthetics to incapacitate guards, meticulous money-laundering plans. Disappeared after the 1994 job. Was never caught. His body is found in the freezer in the chapel cellar – apparently dead since Christmas 1994.
_Chapter: 13, 14_

**Jonathan Terpsichore ("Mr Turps")**
Presents himself as Boht's nephew, a banker in New York. Hired Klee via Pausing to guard the chapel. Frail-looking on video call, sandy hair, blue eyes, always in business attire. By Chapter 14 it is confirmed he does not exist – someone has assumed this name as a false identity. The real person with this name is not the client.
_Chapters: 10, 12, 14_

**Aimee Finch**
A journalist. Four foot eleven, seven stone. Known for being able to look after herself (had poked men in the eye, kneed one in the balls). Found beaten to death in the car park of the Isambard Kingdom Brunel pub in Muswell Hill – her skull smashed with an unusual spanner, bound with string and glue, very hard to get prints from. Her murder on 17 July 1996 was Klee's first day on the job. Became the most expensive, most investigated, and still unsolved murder investigation in Metropolitan Police history. The case Jackie Spanner refused to stop investigating – which is likely what got him killed.
_Chapters: 1, 7_

## The Criminal Underworld

**Bernie Gersten ("Big G")**
One of Klee's closest criminal contacts. Operated clubs in Birmingham (a decision he regrets). Sits in a very dark office. A man to be careful with – there was once a moment when it looked like he might throw Klee in a canal ("That was all a misunderstanding. They'd sorted it out. They'd laughed about it later. Well, Bernie had laughed."). Recommended Preston Vanz for security work.
_Chapters: 11_

**Preston Vanz**
Security and surveillance specialist. Dreadlocks. Techno wizard. Was in high-security prison because he kept breaking out of open prison and hacked the prison CCTV. Son of Dalton Vanz. Mrs Ting takes an immediate shine to him and plies him with tea and cake. Pausing's plan at the end involves using Preston as bait — Klee is told to call him and take the car out of the garage.
_Chapters: 11, 14_

**Dalton Vanz**
Preston's father. Now retired. Was the security man Bernie Gersten used to use.
_Chapter: 11_

**Div**
Colour-blind car dealer with cars on a forecourt. Former car thief who was a rising star in that community before his wife Nav straightened him out. Gave Klee the lime-green muscle car ("Key Lime Pie" – it has flames down the side). The car's actual owner is in the Whittington Hospital, deep in a coma after going through his windscreen.
_Chapters: 2, 6_

**Nav**
Div's wife. The reason Div is not in jail. Said the lime-green car was "bright."
_Chapter: 2_

**Banks**
Mentioned in passing: "What happened to Klee? Did Banks and his boys finally catch up with him?" Appears to be someone with authority or muscle who is looking for Klee.
_Chapter: 5_

**Mohammed Hossain**
Owner of the Crystal Palace Tandoori and Grill. Delivered Jackie Spanner's final meal (king prawn biriyani) himself on the night Jackie was killed. One of the few people who can give a timeline for Jackie's last hours.
_Chapter: 1_

## Minor / Background Characters

**Elaine Sketchley**
Someone Klee slept with. Described as a "bad idea."
_Chapter: 12_

**Henry**
Someone in Klee's circle who would know whether the chapel antiques are valuable. Klee doesn't call him because "Henry would tell everybody where he was."
_Chapter: 5_

**Maggie**
Pausing's corgi. Left behind by a previous partner.
_Chapter: 4_

**Kevin ("Jewish Kevin")**
The friend whose live-in security guard business Pausing is covering. Rediscovered his Jewish heritage after his divorce, came into money (in the wrong order for the ex-wife), went to the south of France and met someone. Away indefinitely.
_Chapter: 4_

**Vanessa**
A veteran stripper. Her observation that tips were dropping off – because her regulars were going less often and spending less – was the insight behind the "stripper theory" of predicting recessions. Had a speciality act with counter-rotating tassels. Told young economist Bernard she was hanging up her pasties.
_Chapter: 13_

**Bernard**
The young economist who frequented strip clubs, befriended Vanessa, and used her declining tips as a recession indicator. Wrote a book called *Stripper Theory*. Won a Nobel Prize.
_Chapter: 13_
